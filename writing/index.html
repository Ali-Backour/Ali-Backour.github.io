The Silence of Nurath

I wrote this for my MIT class Political Utopias and Dystopias (17.517) in Spring 2026.

My mom would be proud of me… I am not afraid of the dark anymore. My five-year-old self would never guess that I, Arelan, would sleep in this dark chamber of the cave at night. I guess it doesn’t really matter that it is night; it is always night here.
